# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Anything Download a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others’ private information without consent
- Encouraging copyright infringement, DRM circumvention, or unauthorized access
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of
Conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported by opening a private security or maintainer contact channel as
described in `SECURITY.md`.
